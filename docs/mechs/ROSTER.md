# THE ROSTER: 14 mech heroes (design, not built yet)

PAPER PLANE and NIGHT SHIFT exist (docs/mechs/PLAY.md). The 12 below are new. Each has a role, armour, speed, a main
weapon, two abilities (Shift / E), an ultimate (Q, charged by damage dealt), a passive and its jank. Every number is a
default; the server judges all of it, like MECH DROP (#310). The look brief is for the mech art session; the owner makes
the meshes in Tripo. All names and designs are original.

**Speed** is ground speed in m/s; the current mechs are about 16.

### WRECKING BALL (dive tank)
- **Main:** ram. Rolling into people hurts by speed (up to 70).
- **Shift:** chain grapple. Swing from any wall or roof, like the killstreak.
- **E:** ground slam. Higher means bigger, and the slam flings.
- **Q:** PILE DRIVER. Shoots up 30 m, then drops anywhere you aim: 200 at the centre, 9 m blast.
- **Passive:** ignores knockback while rolling.
- **Jank:** the pilot sits in a hamster cage inside and spins with every roll; the camera shakes accordingly.
- **Look:** 4 m steel sphere, a riveted band and a chain, yellow hazard stripes, a little pilot cage visible through a
  window. Chunky, readable at 100 m.

### OVERDRIVE (bruiser)
- **Main:** rocket punches. A charged one dashes, as the rig's does.
- **Shift:** leap.
- **E:** quickhack. Fries grapples and stops a mech's abilities for 2 s.
- **Q:** OVERHEAT. 8 s of double punch speed and a fire trail; afterwards it vents for 2 s, stuck.
- **Passive:** blocks from the front with fists up (RMB).
- **Jank:** overheating sets its own paint on fire, and the steam whistle screams.
- **Look:** the Overdrive Rig grown to 5 m. Scrap plates, huge forearms, exhaust stacks on the back, rust orange and black.

### BIN DAY (area denial)
- **Main:** trash mortar. Lobbed bags splash 40 and leave litter that slows people.
- **Shift:** lid hop. Flips its lid and pops 8 m up.
- **E:** stink cloud. A 6 m cloud: 12/s, and it blurs vision.
- **Q:** COMPACTOR. Sucks in everything within 7 m, crushes it (120), and spits out a cube.
- **Passive:** loot it drives over gets vacuumed into its bin.
- **Jank:** recycling sorted wrong gives a "CONTAMINATED" debuff to whoever it hits.
- **Look:** a 4.5 m walking wheelie bin, legs from the wheel axle, a lid that opens like a mouth. Toxic green body, a
  pink "07" stencil.

### VENDING MACHINE (support)
- **Main:** coin turret. Rapid coins, 9 each.
- **Shift:** dispense. Drops a Jank Cola for the squad: 40 HP or armour, plus a 3 s speed boost (3 stored).
- **E:** OUT OF ORDER. A shield wall, 600 HP, 8 m wide, with a sign on it.
- **Q:** RESTOCK. Every ally within 15 m gets 250 armour and refilled abilities.
- **Passive:** a 10% chance an ability "gets stuck" and comes out twice.
- **Jank:** it can be kicked by allies to unstick it.
- **Look:** a 5 m vending machine on stubby legs, a glowing front panel of drinks, a coin slot for a face. Cyan and pink,
  with a lit marquee.

### CLAW MACHINE (control)
- **Main:** claw jab. Short range, 30, pulls a little.
- **Shift:** joystick strafe. A fast sidestep.
- **E:** grab. A claw on a cable grabs one target within 25 m and holds it in the air for 1.5 s.
- **Q:** YOU WIN NOTHING. Grab, carry for 4 s, and drop them where you aim: off a cliff, into the sea, into your squad.
- **Passive:** grabbed targets can't shoot.
- **Jank:** 1 in 6 grabs slip, and the claw drops them on its own head.
- **Look:** a 5.5 m glass arcade cabinet walking on four legs, a giant claw on a gantry, plushies inside. Pink and yellow,
  with chase lights.

### TROLLEY (speed)
- **Main:** tin-can toss. Bouncy cans, 22 each.
- **Shift:** drift. Slides sideways; the drift builds a ram charge.
- **E:** trolley push. Rams with the charge: up to 80, a big shove.
- **Q:** CLEAN UP AISLE 5. 6 s of top speed; it scoops people into the basket and dumps them.
- **Passive:** one wonky wheel. It always pulls a little left (cosmetic plus a tiny steer).
- **Jank:** a pound coin lock: allies can ride in the basket.
- **Look:** a 4 m shopping trolley mech, chrome mesh, a red handle, a tiny pilot standing at the handle. Squeaky.

### ROOMBA (small and fast)
- **Main:** bumper zap. A short-range arc, 15 a tick.
- **Shift:** bump charge. Dashes, bounces off walls and hits again on each bounce.
- **E:** mini-roombas. 4 little bots chase targets and zap them (8 each).
- **Q:** SUCK MODE. Pulls in bullets (blocks projectiles in front) and loot; blows them out as a blast (damage = what it ate).
- **Passive:** tiny hitbox (2.5 m tall); it can go under some things.
- **Jank:** it occasionally says "please empty bin" and spins.
- **Look:** a 2.5 m disc on stilt legs with a dome for the pilot, side brushes spinning. White and cyan, with a green
  status light.

### KARAOKE (sonic damage)
- **Main:** soundwave cone. 12 m, 10 a tick, pierces shields.
- **Shift:** stage dive. Leaps forward and lands with a 5 m shockwave.
- **E:** speaker stack. A turret that blasts a cone for 8 s.
- **Q:** MIC DROP. A 12 m blast, 150 at the centre, everyone flung; a lyric callout on the HUD.
- **Passive:** allies near it reload 20% faster (the beat).
- **Jank:** the main weapon slowly drifts off key, and the damage goes up as it does.
- **Look:** a 5 m mech made of speaker cabinets, a giant mic in hand, a disco-ball head. Magenta and gold, with lights
  pulsing to the beat.

### LAUNDROMAT (tank / pull)
- **Main:** pressure jet. A water beam, 14 a tick, pushes people back.
- **Shift:** porthole shield. Blocks from the front; bullets "tumble" inside and come back out on release.
- **E:** soap puddle. A slippery 8 m patch (marble-style slip).
- **Q:** SPIN CYCLE. A 10 m vortex for 5 s: pulls everyone in and spins them, 20/s.
- **Passive:** takes 25% less damage from the front.
- **Jank:** socks fly out of it when hit, and one in every hundred is your victim's.
- **Look:** a 5.5 m front-loader washing machine, the porthole as its chest, sudsy vents. White with cyan trim.

### DEEP FRYER (damage / burn)
- **Main:** batter shot. Gloopy shots, 18, and they slow 20%.
- **Shift:** hot oil slick. A puddle that slips and burns 10/s.
- **E:** basket toss. Throws a fry basket that traps one target for 1 s.
- **Q:** EXTRA CRISPY. A 6 s flamethrower of oil; anything it kills drops as golden nuggets.
- **Passive:** battered (slowed) targets take 15% more damage.
- **Jank:** "smoked too much": standing in its own oil sets its feet on fire.
- **Look:** a 5 m industrial fryer on legs, two baskets for arms, bubbling oil on top. Steel and fry-yellow, with a
  greasy shine.

### PARKING WARDEN (sniper / control)
- **Main:** ticket gun. A charged long shot, 90 at full charge, marks the target for the squad.
- **Shift:** walkie-talkie. Pings every enemy within 40 m for 3 s.
- **E:** wheel clamp. A thrown clamp that roots one target (or a mech) for 2 s.
- **Q:** TOW AWAY. A tow hook drags one target 30 m toward you; if they hit a wall, 120.
- **Passive:** marked targets take 10% more from everyone.
- **Jank:** every hit leaves a parking ticket stuck to them for the rest of the match.
- **Look:** a lanky 6 m mech in a hi-vis tabard and peaked cap, a ticket printer on its arm, a tow hook on its back. Black
  and yellow.

### BOUNCY CASTLE (tank / knockback)
- **Main:** bounce punch. Inflatable fists, 25, big knockback.
- **Shift:** bounce slam. A high jump and a 6 m landing bounce that flings people.
- **E:** deploy castle. A 10 m bouncy castle for 8 s; anyone on it is flung around uncontrollably.
- **Q:** PARTY'S OVER. Inflates to double size for 6 s, with double armour regen and knockback.
- **Passive:** immune to knockback; bullets bounce visibly off it.
- **Jank:** on death it doesn't explode, it deflates with a long farting whine and flies around the map.
- **Look:** a 5.5 m inflatable knight-castle mech, turrets on its shoulders, a soft puffy silhouette. Red, yellow and
  blue, with stitched seams.

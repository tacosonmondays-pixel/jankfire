## 1. Who he is (proposed)

> **THE HEADLINER · LORD OF ENTERTAINMENT** · *the show never ends. that's not a promise.*

- **Name, proposed:** THE HEADLINER (his billing), real name to be decided. Silly-literal, like Mr. Overtime and
  Madam Chair. Alternatives: ENCORE, TOP BILLING, MISTER JINGLES.
- **What he wants:** an audience that never leaves. Everyone in his district is either performing or watching, and
  the web makes sure nobody walks out before the end, which never comes.
- **The spider idea:** the big top is his web. The tightropes, trapezes and the net under the high wire are all silk
  he spun. A laugh is how he feeds: he doesn't need you dead, he needs you clapping.
- **How he talks:** a showman. Short, booming, deadpan, always "ladies, gentlemen, and whatever you are". Every line
  is set up as a joke, and he waits for the laugh. If it doesn't come, the bells stop.

## 2. The Lust (his partner, proposed)

- The other half of the show. *Proposed:* **MADAME VELVET**, who runs the cabaret side of the tent (the Velvet
  Tent): perfume, heart-shaped lights, a kissing booth, love letters. He sells the laugh; she sells the wanting.
- They're partners, not a couple. They split the take 50/50 and argue about it on stage, and they never agree whose
  district it is.

## 3. The district (proposed)

- **Name, proposed:** **The Midway** (the strip of a carnival with all the rides and booths). Alternative: The Big
  Top.
- **Look:** a crashed circus grown into a town. Striped tents patched with billboards, bulb signs, candy-pink and
  yellow lights, a giant spider-web canopy over everything instead of a sky, a cabaret quarter of velvet curtains and
  heart neon.
- **Traversal gimmick, proposed:** **the web.** Silk lines everywhere act as tightropes and trampolines: bounce on
  the net, slide down guy-lines, and grapple the web for free swings.
- **Where it sits, open question:** a new 11th district, or folded into Pinstripe? Pinstripe already has the Don's two
  court jesters (Pip Grinsworth and Long Larry, the Punchlines). *Proposed:* they're on loan from the Headliner, and
  their "Two Jesters" side quest becomes how you first hear about him.

## 4. Boss fight sketch (proposed)

- **THE HEADLINER · LORD OF ENTERTAINMENT** in the big top's centre ring.
- **Phase 1:** he works the crowd from the web overhead, dropping juggling pins (grenades) and pie barrages. You
  shoot the guy-lines to bring sections of web down.
- **Phase 2:** he drops to the ring on his eight legs. He's fast and skittery, the bells give his position away, and
  each leg can be shot off (the leg flies off with a honk). Madame Velvet heckles from her box and throws the
  spotlight on you.
- **The gimmick:** the APPLAUSE meter. The crowd claps for whoever is putting on the better show. Style kills,
  slides and no-scopes fill your side, and if he wins the crowd he heals.
- **Core, proposed:** **Funny Bone** (a bone slot): enemies you make laugh stagger.

## Tone

The game is cartoon jank and it's rated for playing with friends. The lust side is played as **burlesque and
romance-comedy**, never explicit: flirting, perfume, heart eyes, a kissing booth that steals your coupons, a
love-letter side quest, curtains that close at the right moment. Same rule as the gore, which the game keeps comic.

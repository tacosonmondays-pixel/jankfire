## Modifiers

- **Bouncy** `bouncy`: knockback ++ (×1.8)
- **Sticky** `sticky`: projectiles stick and pop after 0.8 s
- **Overdue** `overdue`: +2% damage per second since your last reload, up to +40%
- **Punctual** `punctual`: shots on the beat of the district clock's tick crit
- **Greasy** `greasy`: drops a banana peel on every reload
- **Haunted** `haunted`: fires by itself sometimes (1 in 60 s), usually at an enemy, not always
- **Rubbery** `rubbery`: bullets bounce once
- **Fleshy** `fleshy`: magazine +20% but the gun burps when it reloads
- **Rounded** `rounded`: all damage is rounded to the nearest 5 (up on 7s, down on 13s)
- **On Loan** `on_loan`: +25% damage, but it returns itself to ☺ CORP after 10 minutes in your bag (it's back at a Late Desk for a fee)

## Why you're free
Every lanyard synced its wearer's mind to the Machine all day "for continuity"; the 17:30 backup kept each employee's
last sync. Cale's last sync was 17:29:41, the second Overtime punched Cale's card (a termination, on his clock) and
killed them. The Schedule only loads into recordings with an active employment record, so Cale's came out blank.
**You were fired nineteen seconds before the end of the world, and it's the only reason nobody can tell you what time
it is.** ☺ CORP flagged the recording as PROTOTYPE 0 (SCHEDULE NOT LOADED) and was studying it so it could be deleted
safely; it kept breaking out of the sims, and Dale let it out first.
Revealed: suspected in Act III (C13 label), explained by Hask in Act IV (C20).

## How collecting works (fixed by the Act I tone pass, 2026-11-10)
- **The door slip.** Collectors staple a ☺ CORP slip to the door of whoever they take: COLLECTED, a name, a time to the
  second, BALANCE CARRIED FORWARD. Every act uses these words.
- **The credit.** A collected person is credited against their district's back pay, then the interest is put back on,
  so the balance never goes down. Feedstock is logged by weight.
- **Bev's jars.** Parts in the clinics carry a date and a truck number; a few carry a first name, and those sell for less.
- **Ken Abara** is one man: Dumpling's "mr. abara", "old ken" among the nine taken in m04, and "Kenneth Abara. Liver."
  in Hask's ledger in Act V. His hip was Bev's work and went up on the truck with him.
- **Cruelty is never a reward gag** ([TONE.md](../TONE.md)). Two later boss endings (Doctor Upsell's shower of organs as
  loot; shooting Hask's tagged donor organs) are to be softened when Acts III and V get their tone pass.

## The Big Heart: what it is (owner, 2026-11-11)
The heart on the bone tower at `gut:big_heart` ([the model](../gut/GUT_HEART_MODEL.md)). Nobody built it.
- **It is hearts.** The fallen's, and the living's: to live in the Gut you give it your heart, figuratively and
  literally. It is cut out and kept there, and the district keeps your body running.
- **So nobody truly dies in the Gut.** A body that is killed comes back. The only real death is that person's own
  heart, destroyed in the Big Heart.
- **It cannot be attacked.** Every heart lost from it is a person, and from the ground you cannot tell which.
- **The Lady's heart is at the centre.** The rest grew round it. Destroy it and the whole heart fails, and everyone
  in the district dies with her. That is why nobody moves against her.
- *Proposed, not settled* (HAT_LORD.md): a brass cap on every resident's chest; the count (one thousand seven hundred
  and twelve, and the ground); the feedstock is on the register too; a heart can be withdrawn and never has been.
- **Reveal order.** Act I: it beats, and she offers to keep yours. Act II: what it is. Act III, with the Gristle page:
  why the Gut can do it.

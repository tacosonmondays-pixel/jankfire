## New things this pass invents (small, but they recur)

- **The slip.** Collectors staple a ☺ CORP slip to the door of whoever they take: COLLECTED, a name, a time to the
  second, BALANCE CARRIED FORWARD. Seen in C1, m04 and m05. It needs one small prop.
- **The books.** A collected person is credited against their hill's back pay and the interest is put back on
  (Sister Agenda). Feedstock is logged by weight.
- **The bus queue.** One flyover wall on the Ring Road with eleven shadows burnt onto it. Seen from the air in C1 and
  close up in m02.
- **Tape on the jars.** Bev's stock is labelled with a date and a truck number.

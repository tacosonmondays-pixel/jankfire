# Scary Forest: what still needs modelling

The walk-through (2026-11-12) was played at Too HD from both team spawns, both bomb sites, the clearing, the campsite, the tower (foot and top), the dock, the road, the hunting stand and the property line, plus two high views. It also used `tools/blender/inventory_maps.mjs`. The pictures are in `walkthrough.jpg`.

Already Blender models, so not on this list: the pines, birches, dead trees, ferns and bushes (`make_trees.py`, about 2,500 instanced copies), and the boulders (`rocks_forest`). Everything else on the map is still code boxes: 794 of them (`docs/blender/AUDIT_PVP_MAPS.md`).

The list is ranked by what players see most: the objectives and landmarks first, then the spawns, then props and scenery. Columns:
- **now:** what's there today.
- **plan:** what the model will be.
- **collision:** what happens to the boxes you bump into.
- **size:** the triangle budget.

Every model covers its own boxes, like the lighthouse and the fort: the boxes are tagged and hidden once the model loads, and drawn again if the file is missing. Collision stays boxes unless a line says otherwise. Big structures use the architecture lane (`tools/blender/arch/`, `public/models/arch/`). Small repeated things are instanced props (`p_*.glb`, `propOf()`).

Order of work: 1, 2, 3, then 4 (a hero character at xhigh effort), then the prop batches (5–8), then the rest. Every PR is one landmark or one small batch, on its own `claude/map-forest-<thing>` branch from the latest main, and gets:
- the asset QA gate (0 errors)
- the tests, plus `tools/arch/gamehash.mjs` before and after to show it's look only
- before/after pictures in Too HD plus one Potato
- a patch-notes line in the PR

None of these is a full redo: every structure here can be dressed over its boxes. If the cabin's stepped roof can't be made to read well when drawn over its steps, it becomes item 3b: a real sloped roof, collision included, with a walk test. That would be a gameplay change, said so in its PR.

The in-game "artists" are made-up joke names, not real people.

The generated tracks are original: every melody, riff and arrangement was written for JANKFIRE, and nearly every sound
is synthesized from scratch in Python (`tools/music/jf/`): the kicks, hoovers, acid lines, stabs, drums, the rubber
ducks, kazoo, boings, squeaky toys, cash register, spoons, gavel, slide whistle and the robot "vocals" (formant
synthesis). No samples from other recordings are used.

## Third-party sounds

A few instruments are played through **FluidR3_GM** (the General MIDI soundfont, rendered with FluidSynth): the
brass stabs in Jank Cola Rave, the rave piano and tuba in Rubber Duck Hardcore, the church organ in Hung Jury
Hardcore and the synth bass in Wrecking Ball Warm-Up.

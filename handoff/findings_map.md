# Review findings for the map side (5; some are the same bug reported by two reviewers: fix once)

## 1. [medium] Route B1 (hall -> spice house) is impassable: the Spice House B-side door is walled off by the overlapping window opening; A1/B1 also run straight into a crate
(id bazaar-b1-spicehouse-door-walled, area bots, shared/map.js:833)

**Problem:** The Spice House west wall gets two openings that overlap along z: the door z -28..-25.5 (y 0..2.8) and the upstairs window z -29.4..-26.6 (y 3.85..6.3). wall() assumes openings don't overlap. For the window it adds the below-sill segment, a plaster box at x -30.2..-29.8, y 0..3.85, z -29.4..-26.6, and that box fills most of the door. I dumped the boxes to confirm. The only ground-level gap left is z -26.6..-25.5 (1.1 m; a player is 0.8 m wide). The window is also mostly covered by the segment above the door. B1's waypoints [-26,-26.7] -> [-31,-26.7] -> [-35,-28] (map.js:880) go through the walled part. A bot that has passed [-31,-26.7] heads for [-35,-28] in a straight -x line, slides along the wall to z=-28 and stays there for good. Separately, the A1 and B1 leg [0,30]->[0,2] (map.js:875/880) runs exactly along x=0 into the 1 m crate at (0,0.5,26) (map.js:756). The crate is taller than the 0.6 m step, and with the wish direction exactly -z there is no slide. Only a random target-driven jump gets a bot over it.

**Failure:** Unopposed test (one bot, no enemies, grappling off): the carrier and a plain attacker both stop at (0,0,27) on A1 and B1. Started past the crate, both stop on B1 at (-29.4,0.1,-28) heading to wp#9 [-35,-28] and never plant. A1, A0, A2, B0 and B2 complete. Attackers spawning at 4 of the 7 attacker spawns pick B1 for site B. In 10-minute 10-bot sims, bot carriers sat at (-29.4,0.1,-28) for 80-94 s, so attackers lost those rounds on time.

**Suggested fix:** Make the west-wall openings not overlap (e.g. window z -29.4..-28.2 above a door at z -27.6..-25.5), or make wall() merge overlapping openings. Put the B1 door waypoints on the door's centreline, with one waypoint inside and one outside (e.g. [-27,-26.6], [-32,-26.6]). Move the A1/B1 x=0 leg off the crate line or add a detour waypoint around it.

**Verifier notes:** The suggested fix is correct. I checked it on an in-memory patched map (h/fix1.mjs): window at z -29.4..-28.2, door at z -27.6..-25.5, B1 door waypoints [-27,-26.6] and [-32,-26.6], and the A1/B1 hall leg moved to [3,30],[3,2] (stalls start at x 4.5, the crate ends at x 1). B1 carriers from (0,63), (16,58) and (6,70) planted in about 15 s, and an A1 carrier with hooks off planted in 23 s, on both seeds. If you fix wall() instead, merging openings by z is not enough, because the two openings cover different heights. Split the wall at every opening boundary and, for each span, emit the solid height ranges left after removing every opening that covers it. map.js:833 is the only wall() call with overlapping openings. The client renders the same boxes, so this also fixes the door slit and the mostly covered window that human players see.

## 2. [medium] Spice House west wall: overlapping door openings shrink both doors and pin bots on route B1
(id spice-house-west-door-overlap, area maps-desktop, shared/map.js:833)

**Problem:** The Spice House west wall gets two openings whose z ranges overlap: the ground door (z -28..-25.5, y 0..2.8) and the balcony door added in 0137f56 (z -29.4..-26.6, y 3.85..6.3). wall() assumes openings that don't overlap. It emits the fill under the balcony door (plaster z -29.4..-26.6, y 0..3.85) and the lintel over the ground door (z -28..-25.5, y 2.8..7.2) as solid boxes. Net effect: the ground door to B is open only at z -26.6..-25.5 (1.1 m instead of 2.5 m), and the balcony door only at z -29.4..-28 (1.4 m instead of 2.8 m). Route B1's waypoints (-26,-26.7) and (-31,-26.7) aim at the intended door centre. A bot inside the house also 'reaches' (-31,-26.7) from 2.06 m away (the threshold is 2.6 m, measured in x/z only), so it then walks straight at (-35,-28) into the solid fill and stays there.

**Failure:** playerOverlap() at the intended door centre (x -30, z -27.5 and z -26.75, y 0.12) returns BLOCKED by plaster z -29.4..-26.6 y 0..3.85. Live check: server on :4150, Bots room, Plant & Defuse on Bazaar, 7 bots. At t=67 s the bomb CARRIER (bot 10) and bots 14 and 11 were all stuck for 8+ s at (-29.4, 0.1, -28.0). A deterministic sim of route B1 from (-17,0,-16.5), using the server's bot movement, stays pinned at (-29.40, 0.12, ~-27.9) for 60 s. 4 of the 7 attacker spawns pick B1 for site B, and the carrier's goal is forced. So the bomb is never planted and the round ends on time. Defenders routed through the plaza to retake B hit the same wall.

**Suggested fix:** Stop the openings overlapping: for example put the balcony door at z -29.6..-28.2 and the ground door at z -27.6..-25.5, or make wall() merge overlapping openings. Then move B1's (-26,-26.7)/(-31,-26.7) to the real door centre and re-run a bot route sim.

**Verifier notes:** Separating the openings as suggested is right: balcony z -29.6..-28.2 and ground z -27.6..-25.5 give free centre bands of -29.2..-28.65 and -27.2..-25.95. It is not enough on its own, though: 5/40 sim bots still pinned at (-29.4,0.1,-28.0). The reason is that (-31,-26.7) is still 'reached' from inside the house (1.6 m from x=-29.4, below the 2.6 m threshold), and the next point (-35,-28) drags bots below the door band onto the pillar. Moving those two points to the door centre does not change that. Put the post-door waypoint on the door line, more than 2.6 m past the inner face. With the B1 tail ...,(-25,-21),(-26,-26.6),(-33,-26.6),(-37,-28),(-40,-33), 40/40 reached site B in the sim. Optionally make wall() handle or assert overlapping openings: split the run at every opening edge and union the open y-ranges per piece. A plain merge is wrong because the two openings have different y ranges.

## 3. [medium] Market hall stalls cannot be jumped (awning overhangs the counter): bots following the hall routes get pinned for the whole round
(id hall-stall-bot-trap, area maps-desktop, shared/map.js:748)

**Problem:** Each hall stall is a 1.1 m counter (x±2, z±1.5) under an awning at y 2.6 that overhangs it by 0.4 m on every side (x±2.4, z±1.9). A bot pressed against a counter is already under the awning, so its jump stops after 0.8 m of rise and can never clear 1.1 m. Routes A1 and B1 send bots through the hall in straight lines: (0,56)->(0,30)->(0,2)->(8,-3) or (-8,-8). Combat knockback, grapples and hook-jumps regularly drop a bot on the far side of a stall from its next waypoint, and from there it pushes into the counter for good. The same routes also run the (0,30)->(0,2) segment straight through the 1.0 m crate at (0,26) (line 756). Bots with no target never jump, so they stop at (0,0,27).

**Failure:** Live run, Bots room, Bazaar, Plant & Defuse, JF_BOMB_ROUND=60. The round 3 carrier (bot 3) picked up the bomb at 110 s and hook-jumped through the hall. It landed at (6.3,0,5.9), north of the east stall at z=4, while heading for waypoint (8,-3). It slid to (8.0,0,5.9) and stayed there until the round ended by time at 168 s ('bombev win time'). Sim from (6.3,0,5.9) with a jump every 1.5 s: maximum feet height 0.80 m (counter top 1.1), still at (8.00,0.00,5.90) after 20 s. Sim without jumps: bots on A1/B1 stop at (0,0,27) against the crate.

**Suggested fix:** Make stalls passable or keep bots off them. Either raise the awnings to >= 2.95 m so a counter can be jumped and stood on, or add hall-centre waypoints and move the (0,26) crate off the route line. Better still, give routeTo an unstuck rule: re-pick the nearest reachable waypoint or sidestep when there is no progress for ~1 s.

**Verifier notes:** Raising the awnings (and posts) to ≥~2.95 m lets bots that have an enemy alive jump onto the 1.1 m counter and walk over it. Bots with no target still don't jump. Hall-centre waypoints only help if the point after (0,2) sits in the centre lane south of the first stall (|x|<4, z<2, e.g. (0,-1)), so a bot in a pocket slides out along the counter. Moving the crate is unnecessary. The robust fix is a no-progress rule in routeTo/stepBot: if the distance to the waypoint hasn't dropped ~0.5 m in ~1.5 s, sidestep along the blocking face or re-pick the nearest waypoint that has a clear chest-height raycast. Also reset ai.route/ai.ri on respawn, because kept indices beeline respawned bots into these stalls.

## 4. [medium] Route A1's elevated Bridge waypoints pull ground-level bots into the Stable's 3.4 m west wall
(id bridge-route-ground-trap, area maps-desktop, shared/map.js:875)

**Problem:** Route A1 reaches the Bridge through (18,-10) and (18,-14), which sit on the 3.4 m landing, then (24,-20) on the 3.4 m Stable roof. Waypoints count as reached by x/z distance alone (< 2.6 m). So a bot at ground level beside the landing, for example coming from the plaza or knocked off the stairs, 'reaches' (18,-14) from below. It then walks straight for (24,-20), slides along the Stable's west face (x=20) and stops at (19.6, 0, -20), where its direction becomes pure +x into a 3.4 m wall it can't jump.

**Failure:** Live run: bot 10 sat at (19.6,0.0,-20.0) at 26 s, and again as the bomb CARRIER at (19.6,0.8,-20.1) from about 119 s, so no plant that round. A deterministic sim using the server's routeTo and bot movement, starting at ground level at (12,0,-14) or (13,0,-18), ends pinned at exactly (19.60,0.00,-20.00) after 20 s while heading for wp#7 [24,-20].

**Suggested fix:** Keep elevated waypoints out of reach from the ground. Add a waypoint at the stair foot that must be reached first. Or have routeTo ignore waypoints more than ~1.5 m above or below the bot and fall back to the nearest one on its own level. Or re-route when there is no progress.

**Verifier notes:** Nav points are [x,z] with no height, so 'ignore waypoints more than 1.5 m above or below' first needs a y per point, either authored or found by a downward raycast at map load. Use that y in two places: when choosing the starting waypoint (the nearest same-level point, e.g. (8,-3) or (18,-2.5) for a plaza bot, so it takes the stairs), and in the reach test (|dy| < ~1.2). Adding a stair-foot point alone does nothing: (18,-2.5) already exists, and the initial pick skips it. Keep a no-progress watchdog as a backstop. A map-only alternative: a step staircase (steps ≤0.6 m) up the Stable's west face near z -20.

## 5. [low] The Gate's 'hole up high you can jump through' is 1.2 m tall: no player can pass it
(id gate-hole-impassable, area maps-desktop, shared/map.js:789)

**Problem:** The Gate wall's second opening is x 51..53.5, y 1.9..3.1. That is 1.2 m tall, but the player hitbox is 1.8 m (PH.height, constant even when crouching), so nothing can get through. The hole also sits entirely over the canal (x 50..56): its bottom is 3.1 m above the canal water and 1.9 m above street level, while the maximum jump is about 1.63 m (jump 9.2, gravity 26). The shortcut into A that the comment advertises doesn't exist; it works only as a window.

**Failure:** Sweeping x 50.5..54 (step 0.05) and y 1.5..3.2 (step 0.02) in the wall plane z=-6 with playerOverlap() finds 0 positions where a player fits. A player who jumps at it from the canal or the street just hits the brick.

**Suggested fix:** Make the opening at least 1.9 m tall (for example y 1.3..3.3) and put solid footing under it within jump reach (a ledge or crate whose top is <= ~1.5 m below the hole's bottom). Or call it a window in the comment.

**Verifier notes:** Either call it a window in the comment, or make it a real passage. The opening needs to be ≥~2.2 m tall (e.g. y 1.2..3.4), so a jump from street level (apex 1.55) can enter without bumping the lintel and a 1.8 m player fits on the 1 m-deep sill. The canal side also needs footing with its top ≥ ~-0.2, since the water is at -1.2.
